# Context
The current TypeSafe judge needs to robustly evaluate and repair step-by-step plans generated by less capable AI models. To achieve this, we will research state-of-the-art repo patterns for multi-agent plan validation and red-teaming, synthesize these findings, and apply them to harden the evaluator against hallucinations and logical gaps.
